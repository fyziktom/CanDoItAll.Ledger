# Economy Simulator Stabilization v19

## Stable

- Matrix v8 is the regression baseline for shared-resource, finite productive-capacity, exchange/market, Smith market/campaign, scenario-pack bridge, and WebGL observer rows.
- Stress campaigns now emit time-series evidence for commons and finite-capacity surfaces, including stable and tragedy/stress paths.
- Driver registrations expose capability metadata for stress, bridge, stochastic seed, long-run, visualization, and stop-condition support.
- Scenario-pack bridge mapping is provider-owned and family-aware.
- Rule policy evaluation emits lifecycle metadata for owner, scope, expected benefit, complexity delta, validation cost, enforcement cost, simplification path, and retirement condition.
- Large-screen WebGL evidence verifies pose and symbol binding at 1440x1100 with zero failed GLB requests and zero primitive fallbacks.

## Bounded

- Economic claims are closed by headless matrix, invariants, oracles, deterministic replay, and artifact hashes. WebGL remains observer evidence only.
- Stress campaigns use small deterministic parameter surfaces suitable for local and CI evidence, not exhaustive research sweeps.
- Browser evidence is large-screen-only; small, medium, mobile, and narrow layouts remain out of scope for this simulator surface.
- Artifact fetchability is validated through product evidence packages once changes are committed.

## Exploratory

- Smith campaign rows remain inference/no-claim where no economic oracle is declared.
- Additional scenario packs can be added, but must declare driver/bridge ownership rather than relying on generic runtime branches.
- Stochastic campaigns need richer seed sets and confidence intervals before being used as research evidence.

## Blocked Or Deferred

- Remote clean-checkout verification of the v19 completed proof requires a commit containing this proof tree.
- Components changes remain out of scope unless a generic WebGL bug is proven with a minimal reproducer.
- Larger institutional learning, adaptive policy search, and richer cross-agent incentives belong to a follow-up research cycle.

## Economic Governance Notes

- Scarce resources modeled in this layer include common-pool resource stock, productive capacity, demand absorption, participant balances, runtime budget, and evidence-review attention.
- Actors include drivers, scenario-pack providers, bridge providers, oracles, WebGL observers, and future research users.
- Incentives are separated: drivers own transition semantics, packs own scenario vocabulary, bridge providers own family mapping, and evidence artifacts own claims.
- Externalities are visible through collapse, scarcity, concentration, starvation, non-convergence, and budget exhaustion signals.
- New rules carry lifecycle cost metadata and must have simplification and retirement conditions.

## Next Cycle

- Add stochastic campaign envelopes with explicit seed budgets.
- Add richer institutional drivers and policy learning surfaces.
- Expand scenario-pack source manifests with signed/versioned descriptors.
- Promote only repeatedly proven local rules into broader policy, with owner, validation cost, and sunset criteria.
